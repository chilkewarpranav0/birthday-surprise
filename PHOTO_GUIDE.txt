PHOTO GUIDE — adding your own memories ♡

1. Open the folder named "assets".
2. Open the folder named "photos".
3. Copy your pictures into this photos folder.
4. Open EDIT_HERE.js with Notepad (Windows) or TextEdit (Mac).
5. Find the section called photos.
6. Change photo-1.jpg, photo-2.jpg, etc. to the exact filenames you added.
   Example: if your picture is named beach-day.png, change:
   { file: "photo-1.jpg", caption: "..." }
   to:
   { file: "beach-day.png", caption: "Our beach day ♡" }
7. Change the captions between the quotation marks.
8. Save EDIT_HERE.js, then refresh index.html in Chrome.

Tips:
- JPG, JPEG, PNG and WEBP photos work in modern browsers.
- Keep filenames simple (letters, numbers, hyphens; avoid unusual symbols).
- You can add or remove photo entries by copying or deleting a whole line
  inside the photos list in EDIT_HERE.js. Keep commas between entries.
- If a photo does not appear, double-check spelling, extension and capital letters.

MUSIC:
Put your legally usable MP3 in assets/music/. Set musicFile in EDIT_HERE.js
to its exact filename, for example "our-song.mp3". Music starts only after
the visitor presses the Music button. Use music you created, licensed, or
have permission to share.

PASSWORD:
In EDIT_HERE.js, change password.enabled to true and password.value to
your chosen password. This is a simple surprise-screen lock only. It is not
secure encryption and does not hide the website's files from a technical user.
